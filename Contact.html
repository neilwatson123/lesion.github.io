PLEASE NOTE - this site is not medical advice

This is a free educational website to help students and doctors-in-training better understand neurological diagnosis. It is not a formal guideline on how to investigate and treat medical conditions. If you are a practising doctor, please follow relevant guidelines and seek support from senior colleagues.

It is not medical advice for anybody with symptoms, an existing neurological disorder, or concern that they may have a neurological problem.

If you think you might have a neurological problem, see a trained professional, following whatever service pathways are available in your area. Please do not contact me for medical advice - I will not answer.
