Case 11. Progressive communication difficulties

Case Where? What? Formulation Outcome

Clinical formulation

Subacute, progressive, mixed dysphasia and right homonymous inferior quadrantanopia, likely due to a tumour affecting the left hemispheric subcortical regions, likely including parietal and temporal lobes - affecting pathways involved in language and the superior optic radiation.
