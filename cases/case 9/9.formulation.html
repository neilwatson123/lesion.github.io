Case 9. Weakness and tingling

Case Where? What? Formulation Outcome

Clinical formulation

Acute right-sided hemibody sensorimotor syndrome in a patient with significant hypertension and vascular risk factors. Likely due to a lacunar stroke affecting posterior limb of internal capsule; alternatively corona radiata fibres (sparing face). A small haemorrhage is also possible.

Outcome
