Case 15 - Dizziness and clumsiness

Case Where? What? Formulation Outcome

Clinical formulation

Acute ataxia and vertigo, evolving over weeks, with lateralised signs suggesting lesion in left cerebellar hemisphere or deep white matter, or its inflow/outflow tracts in the peduncles and brainstem. The chief suspicion is demyelination, and the second possibility is a space-occupying lesion.
