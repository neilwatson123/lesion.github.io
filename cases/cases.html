CASES

The cases are listed below. You can work through them in any order you like - but later cases will tend to assume you have read earlier ones, and not repeat the explanation of the underlying anatomy and clinical features in the same depth.

You can also return to each case at a later stage if you want to revisit it.

When you read the case information, try to think for yourself what the likely diagnosis is - and why - before moving on to the next sections. Take a moment to write down your thoughts, then see how your thought process and answer compares to the worked-through case.
