Case 2. Clumsy hand

Case Where? What? Formulation Outcome

Clinical formulation

Acute, progressive right upper limb ataxia, reflecting a right cerebellar hemispheric lesion. Most likely aetiologies are demyelination, small haemorrhage, or tumour. Infection is possible but less likely (e.g. abscess).
