Case 24 - Dizziness and slurred speech

Case Where? What? Formulation Outcome

Clinical formulation

Acute-onset vertigo with gait and right-sided limb ataxia and dysarthric speech, peaking and resolving in under an hour, but with persistent minor gait ataxia. Likely due to an ischaemic stroke affecting the upper central and right paracentral cerebellum.
