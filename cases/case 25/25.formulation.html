Painful visual loss

Case Where? What? Formulation Outcome

Clinical formulation

23 year old man with fulminant monocular visual loss and headache due to optic neuritis, most likely inflammatory, and less likely due to infection or a compressive mass lesion. If inflammatory, the severity raises suspicion for atypical causes.
