Case 6. Headache and arm movements during pregnancy

Case Where? What? Formulation Outcome

Clinical formulation

Acute headache and focal seizures in third-trimester pregnancy, with localisation to motor areas in left frontal lobe. Main diagnostic suspicion is of a venous sinus thrombosis, though other possibilities include small haemorrhage, a brain infection or a tumour.
