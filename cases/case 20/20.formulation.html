Case 20 - Facial twitching

Case Where? What? Formulation Outcome

Clinical formulation

Right hemifacial spasm - potentially idiopathic although a lesion at the root exit zone (neurovascular conflict most likely) or within the pons is possible. Imaging is worthwhile, including vascular sequences.
